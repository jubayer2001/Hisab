## What is new in Hisab 2.0

- **Bengali.** Tap the language button at the top of Home to switch the whole app between English and Bengali. More has Language and text: a bigger text size (Large or Extra large) and Bengali or English digits. The nightly reminders follow the language too. A few rarely seen messages may still appear in English; they are being translated.
- **Jump to any date.** In Reports and History, tap the date to open a small calendar and pick a day, a week (Saturday to Friday), a month or a year. History also has a new Week filter.
- **See what each category cost.** Tap the wheel or a category in Reports to list everything spent on it for the period.
- **Savings goals on Home.** A goal's progress shows on Home once you have one.
- **Make Home your own.** Home notices (backup, income, budget, bills, Dhar) can be hidden from More, and you can choose one extra detail beside "Spent today": budget status, this week, daily average or a bill due soon. Nothing is shown unless you choose it.
- **Cleaner figures.** The figures on the Home and Reports cards line up in equal columns and adapt to the phone's width, so labels are never cut off.
- **Learn the app.** After updating, a short "what is new" pager opens once. New users are offered a quick tour, and More has Help and tutorials: a short animated guide for every feature, also reachable from the ? button on each feature's screen.

The fire streak chip on Home has been replaced by the language button.
