# Hisab privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Hisab is a personal expense tracker for Android. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your phone, and nothing is shared with anyone.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter (expenses, income, categories, budgets, savings goals, dhar records, your name and reminder settings) is stored **only on your phone**, in the app's private storage. The app works fully without an internet connection and without an account. Hisab has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Optional: Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Hisab keeps a **backup copy** of your data in your own Google Drive, so you can get it back if you delete the app or change phones.

- Hisab asks for one Google permission: access to its **own hidden app folder** in your Drive (`drive.appdata`). It cannot see, open, change or delete any of your other Drive files, and no other app can open that folder.
- Hisab also receives your Google account's **email address**, only to show you which account is signed in. It is not stored anywhere except on your phone, and it is not sent to the developer or any other party.
- The backup is uploaded directly from your phone to your Google Drive. It does not pass through any server owned by the developer, because there is none.
- Signing in is optional. If you never sign in, nothing is ever sent from your phone.

## Who can see your data

Only you. The developer cannot see your data, your backup or your Google account. Your data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

## Deleting your data

- **On your phone:** uninstalling Hisab deletes all data stored on the phone.
- **In Google Drive:** open Google Drive in a browser, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Hisab, and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can also remove Hisab's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
- Signing out inside Hisab stops backups but does not delete the existing copy in Drive.

## Children

Hisab is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
